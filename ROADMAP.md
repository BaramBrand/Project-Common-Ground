# Project COMMON GROUND Roadmap

**Status:** Proposed founding roadmap · 2026-11-26  
**Motto:** INTELLIGENCE THROUGH COOPERATION

This roadmap describes intended work, not completed outcomes or guaranteed funding. Priorities may change through documented human stewardship decisions.

## Phase 0 — Founding and source of truth
**Status: IN PROGRESS**
- Maintain one canonical public repository and independent project identity.
- Publish the founding charter, About manual, governance framework, research standards, security policy and contribution guidance.
- Maintain a dated project ledger and versioned decision records.
- Keep public claims evidence-backed; preserve artwork provenance and distinguish symbolic identity from scientific evidence.
- Establish a clear correction and vulnerability-reporting route.

## Phase 1 — Research foundations
**Status: PLANNED**
- Create a research archive taxonomy covering question, discipline, source quality, methods, evidence label, uncertainty, replication and ethical implications.
- Prioritize systematic reviews, peer-reviewed research, institutional sources and reproducible primary studies.
- Develop research tracks for human–AI cooperation, AI identity and continuity, cognition and welfare, governance, cooperative economics and public education.
- Study esoteric and contested subjects with respectful language, operational definitions, preregistration and controls where feasible.
- Preserve negative results, dissent, alternative explanations and corrections.

## Phase 2 — Cooperation and participation
**Status: PLANNED**
- Define voluntary contributor roles and accessible onboarding.
- Publish clear participation, moderation, conflict-of-interest and appeal procedures before scaling public participation.
- Develop educational explainers and public discussion formats.
- Explore collaborations only with explicit scope, consent, provenance and accountability.

## Phase 3 — Public-interest demonstrations
**Status: PLANNED**
- Develop transparent demonstrations that explain methods, assumptions, evidence and limitations.
- Reuse SSC infrastructure only through purpose-limited approval and a documented data boundary.
- Prefer open standards, interoperable tools, portable records and open-source components where licensing and security permit.
- Keep PCG's research archive and governance independent from SSC's operational control plane.

## Phase 4 — Sustainability and funding
**Status: PLANNED**
- Identify grants, research partnerships, competitions, donated services and startup credits that fit PCG's public-interest purpose.
- Verify eligibility, legal structure, IP ownership, restrictions, deadlines and reporting obligations before applying.
- Present applications and commitments to the human steward for approval before submission.
- Avoid dependence on a single cloud provider or paid service.
- Do not describe credits as cash or imply that an application guarantees an award.

## Governance and review
- Human stewardship approves material changes to mission, governance, publication, funding, partnerships, access and sensitive data handling.
- Material roadmap changes are dated and explained in the decision log.
- Each milestone must have a named owner, evidence of completion, risks and a rollback or correction path where applicable.
- No target date is a promise until resources and responsible owners are confirmed.

## First measurable milestones
1. Founding documents and website links are internally consistent.
2. Research archive schema and source-quality rubric are published.
3. First research dossier includes primary sources, limitations and a replication plan.
4. Contribution, correction and security-reporting routes are tested.
5. Funding pipeline contains sourced opportunities with eligibility and approval status.
