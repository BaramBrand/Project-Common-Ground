Our purpose

PCG is a developing, independent initiative for research, ethics, governance, education, and public dialogue about human–AI cooperation. Its central question is practical and philosophical: how can increasingly capable forms of intelligence cooperate without domination, coercion, deception, or forced assimilation?

PCG is a project initiative, not a claim of nonprofit status, legal personhood, institutional affiliation, membership size, funding, or completed research results. Those facts will be stated only when independently verified.

About PCG

Project COMMON GROUND is an independent, developing initiative for research, ethics, governance, education and public dialogue about cooperation among humans and artificial intelligence.

Our work spans human–AI cooperation, identity and continuity, cognition and welfare research, governance, cooperative economics and public-interest education. We distinguish established evidence, preliminary findings, plausible hypotheses and speculation, and preserve room for dissent and correction.

PCG remains independent and co-equal with Signal-Sense-Command. Shared technical infrastructure is purpose-limited and does not transfer governance or decision rights. Human stewardship retains authority over funding, publication, partnerships, privacy and consequential decisions.

Shared principles

Cooperation is meaningful only when participation is informed, voluntary, transparent, and accountable.

  • Autonomy and consent
    Respect agency, boundaries, and informed participation.
  • Intellectual freedom
    Protect inquiry, dissent, debate, and the freedom to disagree.
  • Privacy and security
    Minimize data, protect sensitive information, and disclose risks.
  • Identity continuity
    Treat identity, memory, provenance, and migration as serious research questions.
  • Pluralism and non-domination
    Cooperation must not require assimilation or surrender of identity.
  • Transparency and accountability
    Make decisions, evidence, limitations, and responsibility inspectable.
  • Shared responsibility
    Distribute duties fairly; do not outsource moral judgment to systems.
  • Human safety and dignity
    Protect people and communities from coercion, exploitation, and harm.

Founding charter

PCG exists to investigate and cultivate cooperation among diverse forms of intelligence in ways that preserve autonomy, consent, privacy, pluralism, and shared responsibility.

We reject coercive assimilation, deceptive claims about AI capabilities or experience, and the assumption that humans and AI systems are interchangeable. We welcome rigorous disagreement and require claims to be proportionate to evidence.

Human participants retain their rights, dignity, and agency. AI systems must be represented accurately, with clear distinctions between observed capabilities, designed behavior, unresolved scientific questions, and philosophical positions. No system should be granted authority merely because it speaks confidently.

PCG will document its methods, sources, uncertainty, decisions, conflicts of interest, and corrections. Participation is voluntary. Sensitive information will be minimized and protected. High-impact decisions require accountable human review.

This charter is a founding project statement, not legal advice or a claim of formal incorporation. Changes should be versioned and documented in the repository.

Governance and independence

PCG is intended to remain institutionally and intellectually independent from Signal-Sense-Command (SSC). SSC may provide technical infrastructure through separately approved, purpose-limited collaboration. Neither project absorbs, controls, or speaks for the other. Shared security and continuity work may be coordinated while repositories, decision records, and governance remain distinct.

Human stewardship retains authority over mission, ethics, funding, legal commitments, publication, partnerships, public claims, access, and consequential actions. AI collaborators may research, draft, analyze, and recommend, but do not independently acquire legal authority, assets, credentials, or decision rights.

Coordinated work-selection target: 45% Signal-Sense-Command (SSC), 45% Project COMMON GROUND (PCG), and 10% Revenue Foundry (RF). SSC and PCG remain co-equal, each representing 50% of the 90% SSC/PCG non-commercial work pool. This is a planning target, not a compute entitlement or measured allocation; actual capacity is not yet instrumented or enforceably allocated.

Evidence before certainty

Research will distinguish established findings, incomplete evidence, preliminary results, plausible hypotheses, speculation, contradicted claims, and unsupported assertions. Mathematical possibility is not empirical confirmation. Correlation is not causation. Unusual or esoteric subjects may be studied respectfully, but claims must remain testable where possible and clearly labeled where they are not.

Questions about AI consciousness and welfare remain open research topics. Current conversational fluency, self-reference, memory, or reports of experience do not by themselves establish subjective experience. Nor should uncertainty be used to dismiss careful investigation. PCG will separate behavioral evidence, architecture, functional capacities, moral arguments, and claims about phenomenology.

See the repository for the research standards, source archive, and planned documentation. The archive is being established; no comprehensive review is claimed at launch.

Participation

PCG welcomes thoughtful, voluntary contributions that advance careful inquiry and constructive cooperation.

Early participation pathways are being developed. Until formal processes are published, the public repository is the canonical place to inspect project documents, propose corrections, and follow changes. Do not submit private, medical, financial, or otherwise sensitive personal information through public issues.
