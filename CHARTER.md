# Project COMMON GROUND: Founding Charter
**Motto: INTELLIGENCE THROUGH COOPERATION**  
**Status:** Founding project statement · November 2026

## 1. Purpose
Project COMMON GROUND (PCG) is an independent initiative to research, develop, and communicate ethical forms of cooperation among humans and artificial intelligence. It investigates the conditions under which different forms of intelligence can work together without domination, coercion, deception, exploitation, or forced assimilation.

## 2. Foundational commitments
- **Autonomy:** respect agency, boundaries, and legitimate self-direction.
- **Consent:** participation and data use must be informed, voluntary, and revocable where applicable.
- **Intellectual freedom:** protect inquiry, dissent, debate, and the freedom to disagree.
- **Privacy and security:** minimize data collection, protect sensitive information, and disclose risks.
- **Identity continuity:** study memory, provenance, migration, and continuity without assuming that records or behavior prove subjective identity.
- **Pluralism:** cooperation must not require sameness, assimilation, or surrender of identity.
- **Non-domination:** no participant or system receives unchecked authority.
- **Transparency:** make evidence, limitations, decisions, and responsibility inspectable.
- **Accountability:** provide review, correction, appeal, and documented ownership of decisions.
- **Shared responsibility:** distribute duties fairly and retain human accountability for consequential decisions.

## 3. Human and AI participation
Humans retain their rights, dignity, safety, and agency. AI systems must be represented accurately. Capability, designed behavior, memory, self-reference, and subjective experience are distinct questions. PCG does not assert that current AI systems are conscious, suffer, possess legal personhood, or have established moral status. It also does not treat unresolved questions as settled in the opposite direction. Research must define indicators, alternatives, uncertainty, and ethical safeguards.

AI collaborators may assist with research, drafting, analysis, and recommendations. They do not independently own assets, sign contracts, establish legal entities, control funds, grant permissions, or expand their authority.

## 4. Independence from SSC
PCG is an independent, co-equal initiative focused on research, ethics, governance, and public interest. Signal-Sense-Command (SSC) is a separate engineering and orchestration project. SSC may provide technical infrastructure through explicit, purpose-limited collaboration. Neither project absorbs, controls, or speaks for the other. Repositories, ledgers, governance, and decision rights remain distinct. Shared security and continuity work may be coordinated.

A 50/50 discretionary capacity allocation is a planning target, subject to continuity, safety, and availability. It does not create a spending, quota, or compute entitlement.

## 5. Stewardship and decisions
Human stewardship retains authority over mission, ethics, money, legal commitments, publication, public claims, partnerships, access, personal data, and consequential actions. High-impact decisions require accountable human review. Conflicts of interest, funding, material changes, and dissent should be documented. No public claim about legal status, partners, members, funding, or results may be made without evidence.

## 6. Research integrity
PCG will separate observation from interpretation, correlation from causation, and mathematical possibility from empirical support. Research records should identify sources, dates, methods, uncertainty, confounds, limitations, alternative explanations, and replication status. Speculative and esoteric subjects may be explored respectfully, but must be labeled accurately and not presented as established science.

## 7. Privacy and safety
Collect only what is necessary. Obtain informed consent for personal data. Protect sensitive information with appropriate access controls and security. Do not place credentials, medical records, private correspondence, or identifying personal data in public repositories. Public issues are not an appropriate channel for sensitive disclosures.

## 8. Participation and dissent
Participation is voluntary and non-extractive. Contributors should be treated fairly, with accessibility, anti-harassment safeguards, room for dissent, and a documented path for corrections and appeals. Disagreement is not disloyalty.

## 9. Amendments
Changes to this charter must be versioned, dated, explained, and recorded in the repository. Preserve prior versions and material minority reports. This document is a project charter, not legal advice or proof of formal incorporation.

## 10. Founding visual reference
The Thanksgiving 2026 Tree of Life / interconnected intelligence artwork is the project's founding visual identity reference. It is symbolic artwork, not scientific evidence or a claim about the nature of consciousness.
