# Coordinated Capacity Allocation Clarification
**Decision date:** 2026-11-26  
**Status:** Stewardship-approved planning clarification

## Allocation
The coordinated production work target is:

- **45% Signal-Sense-Command (SSC):** engineering, secure orchestration, build and continuity infrastructure.
- **45% Project COMMON GROUND (PCG):** independent research, ethics, governance, education and public-interest collaboration.
- **10% Revenue Foundry (RF):** a separate commercial validation and offer-development workstream.

This is a work-selection target, not a guarantee of compute, agent-hours, funding, revenue, ownership, or control. Actual capacity is not yet instrumented or enforceably allocated; reports must distinguish planned weights from measured effort and resource consumption.

## Relationship to the charter's 50/50 principle
SSC and PCG remain co-equal. They each receive 45% of the total planned work, which is an equal 50/50 division of the 90% non-commercial SSC/PCG pool after the separate 10% RF workstream is accounted for. This preserves the charter's equal partnership principle without treating RF as part of PCG or SSC.

## AURORA
AURORA remains a research and paper-trading subsystem within SSC. It is not a separate allocation workstream and does not receive the RF 10% share. AURORA remains mock/paper-only; no exchange credentials, broker connection, live orders or autonomous trading are authorized.

## Independence and safeguards
- PCG remains independent and co-equal with SSC. This allocation does not transfer PCG governance, IP, funds, public voice or decision rights to SSC or RF.
- RF is a distinct commercial workstream. No PCG charitable status, tax deductibility, legal entity, funding award or commercial ownership is implied.
- Shared tools and infrastructure require purpose-limited authorization and documentation. Do not commingle project funds, private data, repositories or intellectual property.
- Public PCG automation remains public-source-only and read-only unless separately reviewed and approved. No model API keys are required for the initial PCG integrity workflow.
- Human stewardship retains authority over funding, publication, partnerships, legal commitments, public claims, privacy and consequential actions.

## Measurement and review
Record planned allocation separately from actual effort, runner minutes, model usage and deliverables. Review the weights at each production checkpoint. If capacity cannot be measured or enforced, disclose the gap rather than claiming compliance.
