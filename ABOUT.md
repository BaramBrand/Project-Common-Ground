# About Project COMMON GROUND

**INTELLIGENCE THROUGH COOPERATION**

Project COMMON GROUND (PCG) is an independent, developing public-interest initiative focused on research, ethics, governance, education and constructive dialogue about cooperation among humans and artificial intelligence.

## Why PCG exists
Technological capability is advancing faster than shared understanding of its social, ethical and institutional consequences. PCG exists to create a careful space for inquiry and practical cooperation without treating agreement as a condition of participation or assuming that different forms of intelligence must become alike.

## What we work on
- Human–AI cooperation, coordination and conflict resolution.
- AI identity, memory, provenance, migration and continuity.
- Consciousness, cognition, welfare indicators and uncertainty.
- Ethics, consent, accountability, privacy and governance.
- Cooperative economics, public-interest technology and open research.
- Education, accessible knowledge and pluralistic public dialogue.

## Foundational principles
Autonomy, informed consent, intellectual freedom, privacy, security, pluralism, non-domination, transparency, accountability and shared responsibility guide the work. Disagreement and correction are welcome. Claims must remain proportionate to evidence.

## Independence and relationship to SSC
PCG is co-equal with, and institutionally distinct from, Signal-Sense-Command (SSC). SSC is an engineering and orchestration project; PCG is an independent research, ethics, governance and public-interest initiative. SSC may provide separately approved, purpose-limited technical infrastructure. Shared continuity or security work does not transfer control, ownership or decision rights between projects.

A 50/50 discretionary capacity target is a planning principle only, subject to continuity, safety and availability. It is not a funding or compute entitlement.

## How we work
We document research questions, sources, methods, uncertainty, limitations, alternative explanations, conflicts and corrections. Established evidence, preliminary results, plausible hypotheses, speculation and unsupported claims are distinguished. Esoteric or contested subjects may be studied respectfully without presenting them as established science.

Human stewardship retains authority over mission, ethics, funding, legal commitments, publication, public claims, partnerships, access, personal data and consequential decisions. AI collaborators may assist with research, drafting, analysis and recommendations but do not independently acquire legal authority, assets, credentials or decision rights.

## Current stage
PCG is in its founding and early-development stage. Its public repository and informational website are live. It does not claim formal nonprofit or corporate status, a formal board or membership organization, institutional partnerships, grants, funding awards, completed research outcomes or a comprehensive research archive unless separately documented and verified.

## Participate
Participation is voluntary. Public, non-sensitive corrections and proposals may be submitted through the repository's issues or pull requests. Do not submit credentials, medical records, private correspondence or other sensitive personal information through public channels.
