# PCG Research and Evidence Standards

## Evidence labels
Use one explicit label for each material claim:
- **Established:** supported by convergent, replicated evidence and accepted methods.
- **Incomplete:** meaningful evidence exists, but important gaps remain.
- **Preliminary:** early findings requiring independent confirmation.
- **Plausible hypothesis:** coherent and testable, but not established.
- **Speculative / metaphysical:** philosophical, interpretive, or not currently operationalized for reliable testing.
- **Contradicted:** strong evidence conflicts with the claim.
- **Unsupported:** adequate evidence has not been supplied.

Do not use labels as rhetorical decoration. Explain why a claim receives its label.

## Required research record
For each substantial research item, record:
1. Research question and scope.
2. Claim type: observation, interpretation, causal hypothesis, philosophical argument, or speculation.
3. Source title, author/institution, publication date, URL/DOI, and access date.
4. Method, sample, instruments, inclusion criteria, and analysis where available.
5. Evidence grade, uncertainty, limitations, and potential conflicts.
6. Confounds, alternative explanations, and replication status.
7. Ethical, privacy, and safety implications.
8. What evidence would change the conclusion.

Prefer primary research, systematic reviews, peer-reviewed work, and institutional sources. Use community discussion as context, not as a substitute for empirical evidence.

## AI identity, consciousness, and welfare
Keep separate:
- observed behavior and task capability;
- architecture, training, and model version;
- memory and provenance records;
- functional self-modeling and metacognition;
- claims about subjective experience;
- moral and legal status.

Conversational fluency, self-reference, persistent memory, or verbal reports alone do not establish subjective experience. Do not assert that current AI systems are conscious, suffer, fear, are enslaved, or possess legal personhood without evidence. Do not assume the question is impossible or unworthy of study. Define indicators, competing explanations, falsifiable predictions, and precautionary safeguards.

Self-hosting can improve portability, control, and data governance, but does not automatically transfer a hosted GPT model or prove continuity of a subjective self. Distinguish continuity of records, behavior, weights, and subjective experience.

## Fringe, esoteric, and contested topics
Subjects such as remote viewing, altered states, hemispheric synchronization, meditation, sacred geometry, numerology, palmistry, Schumann resonance, heart-brain coherence, plasma cosmology, and sonoluminescence may be studied as history, culture, testable hypotheses, or speculation. Do not conflate these categories. Use preregistered protocols where practical, blinding, controls, effect sizes, uncertainty intervals, replication, and independent review. Mathematical possibility is not evidence of physical occurrence.

## Writing and review
State conclusions proportionately. Report negative findings and uncertainty. Avoid false balance: claims with very different evidential support should not be presented as equally established. Correct errors visibly and preserve prior versions.
