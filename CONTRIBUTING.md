# Contributing to Project COMMON GROUND

Thank you for considering a contribution. PCG is an early-stage initiative; contribution processes will evolve transparently.

## Ways to contribute
- Propose corrections to project documents.
- Suggest research questions and credible sources.
- Review evidence quality, methods, and limitations.
- Improve accessibility, documentation, and educational material.
- Identify risks, conflicts, or unintended consequences.

## Standards
- Be respectful and specific. Disagreement is welcome.
- Distinguish evidence, interpretation, hypothesis, and speculation.
- Cite sources and preserve provenance.
- Do not submit credentials, medical records, private correspondence, or sensitive personal information.
- Do not claim to represent PCG unless explicitly authorized.
- Do not imply AI consciousness, legal status, partnerships, funding, or completed results without evidence.

## Issues and pull requests
Use GitHub issues for public, non-sensitive proposals and corrections. Pull requests should explain the purpose, sources, affected documents, risks, and any unresolved questions. Maintainers may request revisions or decline changes that conflict with the charter, privacy, safety, or research standards.

Participation is voluntary. A contribution does not confer governance authority, employment, membership status, or ownership.
