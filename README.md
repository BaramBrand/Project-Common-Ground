# Project COMMON GROUND
**INTELLIGENCE THROUGH COOPERATION**

Project COMMON GROUND (PCG) is an independent, developing initiative for research, ethics, governance, education, and public dialogue about cooperation among humans and artificial intelligence.

## Mission
Explore how diverse forms of intelligence can cooperate while protecting autonomy, consent, intellectual freedom, privacy, identity continuity, pluralism, transparency, accountability, non-domination, and shared responsibility.

## Independence
PCG is co-equal and independent from Signal-Sense-Command (SSC). SSC may provide separately approved technical infrastructure. Neither project absorbs, controls, or speaks for the other. Keep repositories, ledgers, governance, and decision rights distinct. Shared security and continuity may be coordinated.

## Evidence and integrity
PCG distinguishes established findings, incomplete evidence, preliminary results, plausible hypotheses, speculation, contradicted claims, and unsupported assertions. Mathematical possibility is not empirical evidence. Current AI systems are not presumed conscious, suffering, legally personified, or entitled to rights; those are serious questions for careful research, not launch claims.

## Status
This repository is the canonical PCG source repository. The project is being established. No nonprofit or corporate status, institutional partnership, membership count, grant, funding, or completed research result is claimed unless separately verified and documented.

## Stewardship
Human stewardship retains authority over mission, ethics, funding, legal commitments, publication, partnerships, public claims, access, and consequential actions. AI collaborators may assist with research, analysis, drafting, and review but do not independently acquire legal authority, assets, credentials, or decision rights.
